# 🚀 Champion City Mobility SACCO
​A **Fully Digital** Boda Boda SACCO Platform

​**Champion City Mobility** is a **modern**, **technology-driven** Boda Boda SACCO designed to bring **efficiency**, **transparency**, and **smart financial management** to *riders*, *administrators*, and *partners*. 
Our digital platform **centralizes operations**, **automates financial processes**, and **enhances member experience**, moving away from manual paper-based systems.

## ​🔥 Key Features
**​📱 Digital Member Registration**
- ​Online Onboarding: Streamlined sign-up process.
- ​Document Upload: Upload ID, license, and bike details instantly.
- ​Automated IDs: Digital signatures and automated membership number generation.
  
**​💼 Smart Operations Dashboard**
- ​Real-time Analytics: View key performance indicators (KPIs) and operational data.
- ​Activity Tracking: Monitor member activity, location, and station/route management.
- ​Expense Logs: Digital logs for fuel, maintenance, and other expenses.

**💳 Financial Management**
- ​Automated Contributions: Handle daily, weekly, and monthly member contributions automatically.
- ​Loan Management: Full cycle tracking of loan applications, disbursements, and repayment schedules.
- ​Digital Wallet: Integrated digital wallet for seamless rider transactions.
- ​Mpesa Integration: Seamless processing via STK Push, Paybill, and Till number.
​- Receipt Generation: Auto-generated digital receipts (PDF & Thermal print formats).

**🛵 Fleet & Compliance**
- ​Document Tracking: Digital logbook, insurance, and compliance tracking.
​- Reminders: Automated renewal reminders for licenses and PSV permits.
​- Service History: Detailed service records and mileage tracking.

**​🛠️ Admin Tools**
- ​Role Management: Granular staff and role-based permission system.
- ​Disciplinary System: Digital recording and management of disciplinary actions.
- ​Performance: Member performance ratings and analytics.
- ​Communication: Broadcast SMS and push notifications to members.
